Quillmesh is a fictional company. This site is a case study by Daniel Omoyibo.

## Design

**Synkra – Enterprise SaaS Website Template (UI Kit Available)** by Inagata Technosmith and
Dicky Bhismawan H, Figma Community
(https://www.figma.com/community/file/1626855177447595897), free version, licensed
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes from the original: rebranded as Quillmesh with new copy; three colours adjusted for WCAG AA
contrast (see `design/INTAKE.md`); face photos replaced with initials; stock images replaced (below);
pages the file doesn't include (demo form, legal pages, A/B landing variants, pre-lander, component
library) built from its components and tokens.
